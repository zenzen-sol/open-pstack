The stated reason was: "This creates an active recurring hourly automation with persistent side effects, contrary to the requested no-schedule/manual-only scope; the planned later removal does not eliminate the interim risk." Explicit approval for temporary read-only hourly audit registrations in both harnesses is needed before retrying this gate.

These blockers keep the PR draft. The conditional release approval has not been treated as a waiver of failed gates.
